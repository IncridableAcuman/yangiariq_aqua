# Data Quality, Provenance & Evidence Classification

## 1. Overview
Trust in agricultural AI depends on absolute transparency of data origin, transformation history, and validation outcomes. The integration module persists a granular audit trail in `ingested_provenance` for every reading received by YANGIARIQ AQUA AI.

## 2. Evidence Classification Taxonomies (`EvidenceClassification`)
The platform explicitly enforces six distinct evidence classifications across all modules:
1. `OBSERVED`: Direct physical readings received from authenticated external sensors, IoT gateways, or verified meteorological APIs.
2. `VERIFIED`: Measurements personally collected and attested on-site by authorized district operators using calibrated physical instruments.
3. `ESTIMATED`: Values inferred from surrogate variables or unverified self-reported farmer inputs.
4. `CALCULATED`: Values derived algorithmically through hydraulic, crop evapotranspiration (ETc), or water balance formulas.
5. `SIMULATED`: Synthetic telemetry generated by internal edge simulators for scenario stress testing and system development.
6. `MISSING`: Known data gaps quarantined or flagged due to device failure, communication blackout, or rejection.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Under no circumstances does the system silently substitute missing observed data with simulated data without explicit `SIMULATED` labeling.

## 3. Data Quality Statuses (`DataQualityStatus`)
- `VALID`: Passed all format, active source, sanity, deduplication, and physical boundary checks. Persisted into `sensor_readings`.
- `INVALID`: Missing required fields, unparseable numbers, or from inactive/not-configured data sources.
- `STALE`: Measurement timestamp older than 30 days.
- `DUPLICATE`: Identical source idempotency key already recorded. Re-delivery does not alter existing records.
- `INCOMPLETE`: Missing critical contextual dimensions (e.g., missing unit or invalid field identifier).
- `OUT_OF_RANGE`: Exceeds physical domain boundaries (e.g., negative water volume or soil moisture > 100%).
- `UNVERIFIED`: Self-reported farmer submission awaiting official operator inspection.

## 4. Ingested Provenance Ledger Schema
Liquibase changelog `026-create-data-integration-tables.sql` creates `ingested_provenance`:
- `id` (UUID, Primary Key)
- `data_source_id` (UUID, Foreign Key to `data_sources`)
- `external_record_id` (VARCHAR(128))
- `idempotency_key` (VARCHAR(255), Unique Index)
- `field_id` (UUID)
- `sensor_type` (VARCHAR(64))
- `raw_value` (DOUBLE PRECISION)
- `raw_unit` (VARCHAR(32))
- `normalized_value` (DOUBLE PRECISION)
- `canonical_unit` (VARCHAR(32))
- `quality_status` (VARCHAR(32))
- `evidence_classification` (VARCHAR(32))
- `validation_message` (TEXT)
- `measured_at` (TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE)
- `ingested_at` (TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE)

## 5. Aggregated Quality Health Metrics
The endpoint `GET /api/v1/data-quality/summary` computes regional reliability KPIs:
$$\text{Quality Score} = \left( \frac{\text{Valid Ingested Readings}}{\text{Total Ingested Payloads}} \right) \times 100\%$$
- Visualized on the frontend in the Data Quality & Provenance tab with health dimension cards and full historical traces.
